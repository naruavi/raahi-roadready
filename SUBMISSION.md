# Competition summary

Raahi reimagines India’s fragmented driving-licence journey around one outcome: completing the work in a single successful visit. Citizens describe what they need in everyday language, and RoadReady combines renewal, address update, challan clearance, documents, payments, and appointment dependencies into one ordered plan. A preflight check identifies missing or inconsistent information before submission; the prototype demonstrates this by finding and resolving a cross-document name mismatch. Once ready, Raahi creates a QR One-Visit Pass that shares verification status without exposing document numbers. The prototype also supports first-time licence applications, learner-test booking, licence renewal, eChallan lookup and simulated payment, RTO comparison, appointment rescheduling, maps, location sharing, tracking, downloadable receipts, English, and Hindi. All identities, documents, fees, payments, government responses, and integrations use fictional data. Raahi is an independent hackathon prototype, not an official government service. It shows how proactive guidance, once-only data, and privacy-preserving verification can reduce failed applications, repeat visits, and anxiety.
