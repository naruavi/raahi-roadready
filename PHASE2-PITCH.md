# Raahi Phase 2 pitch

## Winning story

The pitch follows one transformation:

> A citizen expecting one simple renewal is about to waste a day. Raahi first
> turns the outcome into one coordinated journey. VisitTwin then rehearses that
> journey, exposes the exact counter where it would fail, fixes the issue once,
> and proves the visit is ready without exposing document numbers.

The story deliberately avoids a feature tour. Minute one establishes the human
cost and shows the existing RoadReady journey. Minute two spends almost all its
time on one differentiator: **VisitTwin, a pre-visit simulation of the service
counter journey**.

## Two-minute narration

### Minute 1 — follow Asha

Meet Asha. Tomorrow, she renews her licence. One simple visit, she thinks.

But her address changed, a challan is pending, and one document shortens her
middle name. Today, that can mean three portals, repeated forms—and another day
away from work.

Asha opens Raahi. Instead of choosing a department, she starts with the outcome
she needs: finish everything in one successful visit.

She says it naturally: “My licence expires next month. I moved. Check my
challans before I go.” That is all Raahi needs.

RoadReady turns her sentence into one sequence: clear the challan, update the
address, renew the licence, then visit the right RTO. Three journeys become one.

### Minute 2 — the turning point

Then VisitTwin rehearses her visit. Counter one clears. Counter two... Stop. Her
names do not match. One lost workday. A 28-kilometre return trip. Eleven days
for another slot.

Asha approves one correction. It flows through every service. VisitTwin runs
again. One, two, three, four—every counter clears.

Only now does Asha receive her RoadReady Pass. The QR proves what is ready and
where she is going, without exposing her licence, address proof, or payment
numbers.

Asha leaves home certain—not hopeful.

Raahi does not digitize the queue. It prevents the failed visit.

## Delivery notes

- Speak to one citizen and one failed visit; do not list every Raahi feature.
- Pause on “Your visit would stop at counter 2.”
- Let the before/after color change carry the VisitTwin explanation.
- End on the category statement, not a generic thank-you.
- Keep the prototype disclaimer visible, but do not interrupt the story with it.
